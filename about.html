Our Beginning

How Steller Events Was Born

Steller Events was founded on a simple but powerful belief: that every occasion — no matter the scale — deserves to be extraordinary. What began as a passion for beautiful gatherings in Lokoja has grown into one of Kogi State's most recognised names in luxury event planning.

"We don't just plan events. We engineer moments that people carry in their hearts for the rest of their lives."

Our founder saw a gap in the market — clients who had big dreams for their celebrations but lacked a trusted partner who could translate vision into reality with precision, elegance, and zero stress. That's exactly the gap Steller Events was built to fill.

From intimate birthday soirées to large-scale government ceremonies at the State House, we have built a reputation for showing up prepared, staying calm under pressure, and delivering results that exceed expectations every single time.
